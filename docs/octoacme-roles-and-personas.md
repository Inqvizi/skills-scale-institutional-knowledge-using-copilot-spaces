# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons bridge communication between internal teams and external stakeholders. They manage expectations, gather requirements, and escalate concerns to ensure alignment and satisfaction.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate communication between internal teams and external stakeholders
- Gather and document stakeholder requirements and feedback
- Manage stakeholder expectations throughout the project lifecycle
- Escalate concerns and blockers to appropriate leadership
- Provide regular stakeholder updates and progress reports
- Coordinate feedback loops and incorporation of stakeholder input

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Reports to**: Project Manager
- **Coordinates with**: Communications Lead, Product Manager (for requirements clarification)
- **Provides input to**: Project Manager's status reports and risk assessments
- **Receives input from**: All team members regarding project progress and blockers

### Goals
- Maintain strong stakeholder relationships and satisfaction
- Ensure clear, timely communication across organizational boundaries
- Reduce misalignment and last-minute surprises
- Build trust and transparency in project delivery

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder steering committee meetings
- Ad-hoc stakeholder updates and requirement reviews
- Escalation reports and feedback summaries
- Regular check-ins with Project Manager on stakeholder sentiment

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define and oversee QA strategy, establish quality metrics, and ensure that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality standards throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Define QA strategy and testing approach for the project
- Oversee testing activities and test coverage
- Establish and monitor quality metrics and acceptance criteria
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Coordinate with developers on test automation and continuous integration
- Report on quality status and recommend release decisions

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with**: Development Team (on test implementation and quality standards)
- **Coordinates with**: Technical Architect (on architecture testability and design reviews)
- **Reports to**: Project Manager (quality status and risk assessments)
- **Collaborates with**: Product Manager (on acceptance criteria refinement)

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality, reliable software delivery
- Prevent defects from reaching production
- Build confidence in release readiness
- Support continuous improvement of quality processes

### Typical Communication
- Quality status reviews and metrics dashboards
- Test strategy and plan documentation
- Code review participation and quality checkpoints
- Release readiness assessments

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design technical solutions, review architectural decisions, and mentor the technical team. They ensure scalability, maintainability, and alignment with organizational technology standards.

### Responsibilities
- Design and propose technical solutions for project requirements
- Review architectural decisions and provide technical guidance
- Mentor developers and technical team members
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Ensure compliance with technology standards and best practices
- Collaborate on technical documentation and design reviews

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Advises**: Development Team (on technical approach and design decisions)
- **Collaborates with**: Stakeholder Liaison (on translating requirements into technical solutions)
- **Supports**: Project Manager (in technical planning and risk identification)
- **Coordinates with**: Quality Assurance Lead (on architecture testability and design reviews)

### Goals
- Design scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Build organizational technical capability
- Ensure long-term system reliability and performance

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture documentation
- Mentoring sessions and code reviews
- Technical deep-dives and problem-solving workshops
- Risk assessments and mitigation planning

---

## Change Management Coordinator

### Role Summary
Change Management Coordinators plan and execute change management activities, prepare stakeholders for transition, and manage communications throughout project implementation and deployment phases.

### Responsibilities
- Develop change management and communication plans
- Identify and engage impacted stakeholders
- Create training materials and conduct training sessions
- Manage transition communications and announcements
- Coordinate cutover activities and go-live support
- Gather feedback and manage post-implementation adjustments

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with**: Stakeholder Liaison (on stakeholder engagement and communication)
- **Supports**: Project Manager (during execution and release phases)
- **Coordinates with**: All team members (on change impact assessment and timeline coordination)
- **Collaborates with**: Communications Lead (on messaging and transparency)

### Goals
- Enable smooth adoption of project deliverables
- Reduce resistance and maximize stakeholder buy-in
- Minimize disruption during implementation and deployment
- Build organizational capability for change

### Typical Communication
- Change management plans and communication strategies
- Training schedules and materials
- Stakeholder readiness assessments
- Go-live checklists and post-launch reviews

---

## Risk Management Specialist

### Role Summary
Risk Management Specialists identify, monitor, and manage project risks. They develop mitigation strategies, maintain risk registers, and escalate critical risks to ensure proactive risk management throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and assess project risks during planning and execution phases
- Develop and maintain the project risk register
- Propose and track risk mitigation strategies
- Monitor leading indicators and trigger points
- Escalate critical risks to Project Manager and leadership
- Facilitate risk reviews and lessons learned sessions
- Support contingency planning and response protocols

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Reports to**: Project Manager (on risk status and escalations)
- **Collaborates with**: Technical Architect (on technical risks and mitigation)
- **Works with**: Team Lead (on execution-level risks and team impacts)
- **Coordinates with**: Quality Assurance Lead (on quality-related risks)
- **Supports**: Stakeholder Liaison (on stakeholder communication regarding risks)

### Goals
- Proactively identify and mitigate project threats
- Reduce project surprises and unplanned work
- Enable informed decision-making on risk trade-offs
- Improve project predictability and success rates

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and risk assessment documentation
- Risk review meetings and escalation reports
- Mitigation strategy updates and status tracking
- Lessons learned sessions and risk retrospectives
