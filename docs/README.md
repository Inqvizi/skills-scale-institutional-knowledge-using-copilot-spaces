# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Welcome

This directory contains the complete project management framework used by OctoAcme to deliver features, services, and integrations with consistency, clarity, and customer focus.

## Overview of OctoAcme Project Management Approach

OctoAcme follows a structured, iterative approach to project management that emphasizes:

- **Customer-first thinking**: Every decision prioritizes customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Breaking work into small, testable increments for faster feedback and learning
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has clearly defined roles (Project Manager, Product Manager, Developers, QA) with transparent responsibilities
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measuring impact and iterating based on evidence rather than assumptions
- **Psychological safety**: Encouraging feedback, learning, and continuous improvement across the team

The OctoAcme lifecycle follows five phases: **Initiation** → **Planning** → **Execution** → **Release** → **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement**. Throughout all phases, we maintain active risk management and stakeholder communication.

## Key Project Management Processes

### 1. **Project Initiation**
Validate that a new initiative has clear business need, defined success metrics, stakeholder alignment, and sufficient resources before committing to planning.

**Key Deliverables**: Project One-pager, Stakeholder list, High-level timeline, Initial risk list

### 2. **Project Planning**
Transform an approved initiative into an actionable plan with prioritized backlog, clear acceptance criteria, and identified dependencies.

**Key Deliverables**: Prioritized backlog, Definition of Done, Release plan, Risk register

### 3. **Execution & Tracking**
Manage day-to-day delivery through standups, sprint planning, CI/CD, quality checks, and blocker escalation.

**Key Activities**: Daily standups, Weekly delivery syncs, Automated testing and linting, Manual QA when needed

### 4. **Release & Deployment**
Standardize how features are released to production with pre-release checklists, deployment verification, and rollback procedures.

**Key Deliverables**: Release notes, Deployment checklist, Post-deploy verification, Incident playbook

### 5. **Retrospective & Continuous Improvement**
After each sprint, release, or milestone, capture learnings and convert them into actionable improvements with clear owners and timelines.

**Key Artifacts**: Retrospective notes, Action items, Impact measurement
